Timagotchi

A Raspberry Pi Zero school schedule display system

Never miss a class again. Timagotchi displays your current period, time remaining, grades from Canvas LMS, and even includes embedded games—all on a tiny 1.44" LCD screen.

Build Your Own

Features

📅

Smart Schedule

Displays current period, progress bars, and time remaining. Supports A/B day scheduling and advisory periods.

🎓

Canvas Integration

View your grades and assignments directly from Canvas LMS. Keep track of your academic progress.

🎨

Customizable Themes

Choose from multiple color themes: Dark, Light, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, and Cyberpunk.

🎮

Built-in Games

Play Tetris or a Doom-style raycaster during breaks. Hidden in the secret menu!

📡

WiFi Enabled

Configure multiple WiFi networks for seamless connectivity at home and school.

⚡

Battery Powered

Run on a portable battery bank. Take your schedule display anywhere.

Configure Your Timagotchi

Enter the 5-digit code displayed on your device to get started.

Where to find your code:

  1. Boot up your Timagotchi device
  2. If no configuration exists, a 5-digit code will appear on screen
  3. Or navigate to: Settings → Configuration Portal
  4. Enter the code above and click Continue
